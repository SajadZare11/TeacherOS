Privacy notice

TeacherOS stores the Telegram account, class settings, saved teaching materials, and teacher-approved records needed to provide the service.

What we do not need

Do not submit learner names, contact details, or sensitive personal information. Evidence analysis is designed for limited, anonymized classroom samples.

Control and deletion

You remain in control of saved materials, evidence, reports, and approvals. Use the in-bot privacy controls or contact the configured owner to request deletion.

Providers and retention

AI requests use the configured provider and contain only the bounded context required for the selected task. Retention follows the product settings and pilot runbook. We do not sell teacher or learner data.
