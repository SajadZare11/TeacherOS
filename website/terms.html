Terms of use

TeacherOS is a teaching assistant, not an autonomous evaluator. Teachers must review generated materials, answer keys, reports, and recommendations before using or sharing them.

Acceptable use

Use TeacherOS for lawful English-teaching preparation. Do not upload confidential, identifying, or harmful content, and do not rely on generated content for high-stakes decisions without independent teacher judgment.

Plans and payments

Free, Pro, and Premium limits and prices are shown in the bot and may change during the pilot. A subscription activates only after the payment server verifies the transaction. Sandbox payments are not real charges.

Availability

This is a progressive pilot. Features may be paused, corrected, or improved while reliability, privacy, and teacher value are measured.
