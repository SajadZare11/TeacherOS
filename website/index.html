Questions

Before you start

TeacherOS is designed to feel familiar from the first message.

Do I need to know prompt engineering?+

No. TeacherOS collects the information step by step and builds the prompt for you.

Which English levels are supported?+

TeacherOS supports all six CEFR levels: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2.

Are my materials saved?+

Yes. Generated lessons, activities, worksheets, and assessments are saved automatically to your private library.

Can I print the materials?+

Yes. Saved resources can be exported as Word documents or printable PDF files.

Where do I use TeacherOS?+

TeacherOS currently runs inside Telegram, so you can use it on your phone or computer without installing another app.
