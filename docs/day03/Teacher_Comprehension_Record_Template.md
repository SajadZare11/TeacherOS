## Pass rule

Pass only when the teacher unaided:

- explains Home and all eight stages;
- identifies evidence as optional and separately consented;
- identifies extraction, diagnosis, and follow-up as teacher-controlled;
- finds Back, reject, recovery, and delete controls;
- identifies the saved next lesson—not upload or generation alone—as loop completion.

Any failed required task makes the session a failure. Revise the wireflow or copy, note the new commit, and test with a fresh eligible teacher. Do not coach during the scored portion or convert confusion into a pass.
