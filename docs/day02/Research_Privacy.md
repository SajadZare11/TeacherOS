# Day 2 Research Privacy Rules

## Never commit

- Teacher names, Telegram handles, phone numbers, email addresses, or employer/school names.
- Audio/video recordings, transcripts, screenshots, or raw interview notes.
- Student names, student contact details, health/disability data, grades tied to identities, or identifiable student work.
- Original lesson materials or reports unless the teacher owns them, explicitly permits storage, and all identifying/copyright-sensitive content is removed.

Store private source material only below the ignored local path `research_private/day02/` using anonymous participant and artifact IDs.

## Allowed in the repository workbook

- Anonymous IDs: `P01`, `INT-01`, `ART-P01-01`.
- De-identified task steps, time estimates, workarounds, consequences, desired outcomes, proof of success, and trust concerns.
- Numeric research scores with explicit anchors.
- Problem codes, aggregate counts, feature decisions, and short paraphrases that cannot identify the teacher or a student.

## Artifact handling

1. Ask permission before viewing or retaining an artifact.
2. Prefer observing without retaining.
3. Require removal of student names and identifying metadata.
4. Assign an anonymous artifact ID.
5. Record only the workflow observation needed for the decision.
6. Honor deletion requests and record deletion outside Git.

## AI use

Do not paste raw interview notes or artifacts into an AI service. If AI is used for synthesis, provide only de-identified structured observations and verify every conclusion against the source IDs. AI may organize evidence; it may not manufacture quotes, scores, repeated pain, willingness to pay, or consent.
